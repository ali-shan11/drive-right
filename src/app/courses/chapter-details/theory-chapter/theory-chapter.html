1.0 Introduction

Driving in Lagos requires not just skill, but extreme patience and anticipation. The Golden Rule of driving is: Concentration, Observation, and Anticipation. Always maintain at least a two-second gap between you and the vehicle in front. In wet conditions, this should be doubled to four seconds.

2.0 Right Way of Rules

At an unmarked T-junction, the vehicle on the major road always has right of way. On a roundabout, traffic already circulating takes priority over entering vehicles. Always yield to pedestrians at designated crossings — LASTMA enforces a ₦5,000 fine for violations.

FRSC Regulations

Under Section 8.4 of the Nigerian Highway Code, driving with a blood alcohol level above 0.05% BAC constitutes a criminal offense, punishable by license suspension and a ₦50,000 fine.

3.0 Lane Discipline on Lagos Expressways

The right lane on a dual carriageway is for overtaking only — return to the left after passing. On the Lagos-Ibadan Expressway, the speed limit is 100km/h. FRSC speed guns are deployed regularly at Sagamu interchange. Keep to marked lanes and avoid straddling.
